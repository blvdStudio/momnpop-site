About us

Who are we?

Mom&Pop is a Belgian food concept creating warm and vibrant places centred around fresh, healthy and, above all, delicious food.

Our ambition is simple: create places where people enjoy coming to eat, meet and take a break, with a strong focus on quality, freshness and enjoyment.

Whether you come for lunch, to work, to catch up with someone or simply to enjoy good food, what matters is the same: it should be good, simple and, above all, Yummy.

Our approach

We believe a good meal can truly make a difference to your day. That's why we prioritise fresh, high-quality ingredients, with particular attention to local and seasonal produce.

Menus that are varied, generous and balanced, with options for every taste and every craving.

Our restaurants

Above all, places where people feel good. Relaxed and welcoming spaces to take a break, share a meal or simply enjoy a good moment.

Our commitment to sustainability

Environmental responsibility is at the heart of our philosophy: eco-friendly packaging, minimal food waste and support for sustainable agricultural practices.

We also encourage our teams and customers to adopt more environmentally friendly eating habits.

FAQ

Good questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we reply within one working day.

How far in advance should I order?

For the webshop, order before 2 pm for next-day delivery. For events, we recommend two to three weeks ahead, and two months for weddings.

Do you handle allergies and dietary needs?

Yes. Every menu includes vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options, and each box comes with a full allergen list.

Where do you deliver?

Only in Ghent for now. For an event outside Ghent, contact us and we'll see what's possible.

Can I change my order after confirming?

You can adjust quantities up to 48 hours before delivery, free of charge.

What happens to leftover food?

We pack leftovers for you or donate them to local partners, in line with our commitment to reduce food waste.
